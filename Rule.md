# RULES
 
Every listing ends up in one of five groups: **Short list**, **Ask first**, **Suspected copy**, **Hidden** (with the reason, can be shown again), or **Info**. Nothing is deleted. *(verify)* = threshold still to be checked. Links go to the listings in Audit.md.
 
## S: Short list
| ID | Rule | Evidence | Wrong when |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Short-list only if it has a permit, is verified, is priced within ±15% of the median (or the gap is explained), is available, and has no open question. | [#20](https://www.propertyfinder.ae/en/plp/rent/apartment-for-rent-dubai-dubai-marina-elite-residence-162792136.html), [#27](https://www.propertyfinder.ae/en/plp/buy/apartment-for-sale-dubai-dubai-marina-elite-residence-159600029.html) | The flat was let yesterday. |
| S2 | "Confirmed by agent" needs the agent's answer and expires after 72 h. | Simulated replies | It is let within the 72 h. |
