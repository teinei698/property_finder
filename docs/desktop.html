Scout: a short list you can trust

A personal assistant inside Property Finder search. You tap a few answers; Scout removes likely bait, merges copies of the same flat, asks agents your questions, and gives you a short list with the real price. The agent stays in the deal.

Try it (in the phone on the left)

  1. Tap Build my short list and answer by tapping.
  2. Pick the questions Scout should send to agents.
  3. Open Why Scout trusts this on any flat: every fact shows its source (portal data, AI reading the ad, AI looking at the photos, rules).
  4. Or open How it works (bottom right) and run one of 4 real cases.

Real and simulated

  • Real: 38 live listings from Elite Residence, Dubai Marina (14 Nov 2026), compared with all 442 live ads in the tower.
  • AI, not live: photo tags from Claude's visual check of the first 3 photos of each listing.
  • Simulated: agent replies. They use only what each real ad states, and mark the rest "to confirm".
